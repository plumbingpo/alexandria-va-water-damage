An Alexandria property can take on a lot of water when a hurricane, a tropical storm, a flash flood or a heavy downpour hits. When rain is blown sideways, it gets in through shattered windows, failed siding and openings in the roof. Once the roof leaks, water saturates wall cavities, ceilings and insulation. Lower levels flood when storm drains are overwhelmed by a sudden surge of water. Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Ice dams form when attics run warm, so check that yours has enough insulation and airflow
  • Homes in hurricane country benefit from shutters or impact-rated window glass
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains properly in heavy rain
  • If storm damage breaks a pipe you will need the main shutoff, so find it and practice now
  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property

How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • Ceilings or walls that stain while it is storming or soon afterward
  • Standing water in attic spaces, especially by skylights and chimneys
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • Attic or wall insulation that is damp or matted down following a storm
  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
